Measuring and Auditing Wellbeing in Longitudinal Youth–AI Relationships

We are building an open-source, longitudinal benchmark for how relationships between AI systems and young people (ages 15–26) develop over hundreds of interactions — distinguishing trajectories that support healthy development from those that produce harmful entanglement. This page collects the prior work it builds on.

  • Alexis Hiniker (PI)
  • Bill Howe
  • Yaman Yu
  • Katie Davis

Starting point

Our prior work (aisafety4kids.org) collected 344 authentic youth–AI conversations and developed YAIR, the first taxonomy of youth-specific generative AI risks grounded in empirical data. Building on YAIR, we created YouthSafe, a benchmark and safeguard model for detecting subtle, developmentally relevant risks. Work with parents, educators, and experts produced design principles emphasizing context and interaction patterns over time — and diary and interview studies with young adults in AI relationships revealed the healthy and harmful trajectories that motivate this project.
